PcapPlusPlus's SIP-request dissector locates the " SIP/" version token inside the request's first line by running a C-string search directly over the packet's raw byte buffer, implicitly assuming that buffer is NUL-terminated. A crafted SIP-over-UDP packet whose captured bytes end (at or very near the tail of the heap-allocated raw-packet buffer) without ever containing the " SIP/" substring and without any NUL byte before the buffer's real end causes that search to keep scanning past the last valid byte of the allocation, producing a heap-buffer-overflow read (ASan reports a 13-byte out-of-bounds read) while the packet is being parsed layer-by-layer from a pcap file.
